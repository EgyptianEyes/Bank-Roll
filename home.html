A EUROPEAN ROULETTE RESEARCH PROJECT

Can the evidence reveal what instinct misses?

Bank-Roll records roulette observations, tests forecasts before outcomes are known and measures what survives scrutiny. It is an experiment in careful reasoning about chance—not a promise that roulette can be beaten.

01 / WHY BANK-ROLL

Streaks feel meaningful. Evidence has to earn that meaning.

Roulette invites intuition: a number looks overdue, a street seems hot, a memorable run feels like a signal. But random sequences naturally contain clusters and gaps. Looking backwards can make noise look persuasive.

We built Bank-Roll to ask a harder question: can carefully recorded outcomes and fair, forward-looking tests identify information a human player would otherwise miss—or does the apparent pattern disappear when measured properly?

02 / MEET BRI

Intelligence that keeps its working visible.

Bank-Roll Intelligence (BRI) is the software behind the observations, forecasts and decisions. Its job is not to sound certain; it is to compare ideas, show the evidence they have and make the limits clear.

01 · OBSERVE

Record the next result

Keep ordered outcomes and their source distinct: human observations, simulator outcomes and temporary test data are not interchangeable.

02 · EVALUATE

Forecast, then score

BRI stores a forecast before the next outcome, scores it afterwards and compares fixed models with a uniform-wheel baseline.

03 · DISCLOSE

Recommend with restraint

When evidence is weak or sparse, BRI can recommend observation. A forecast is not a guaranteed edge or an instruction to bet.

03 / AT THE TABLE

For the player who wants a better record, not a louder hunch.

At a physical table, you watch the wheel and enter the winning number. Bank-Roll records the observation locally, updates BRI's assessment and shows any proposed wager for you to review. You decide whether to act; Bank-Roll never places a bet automatically.

05 / WHAT WE KNOW

What has been tested—and what has not.

BRI evaluates a fixed family of forecasting models using forecasts recorded before outcomes and scores them afterwards. Simulations can test accounting, repeatability and behaviour under synthetic assumptions. Those are useful checks, not proof of a real-world advantage.

37equally likely outcomes in the ideal European-wheel model
12three-number streets in the standard betting layout
0verified live-wheel advantages established by this project

No real-wheel bias, profitable strategy or independently validated live prediction advantage has been established. A better historical score is not, by itself, evidence of future profit.

06 / THE CONTINUING EXPERIMENT

A question worth testing carefully.

Bank-Roll is an evolving research project. Models and decisions remain open to evaluation; genuine progress depends on eligible observations, transparent methods and evidence that holds up beyond the data that inspired a hypothesis.
